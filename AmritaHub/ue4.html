About

Participants, researchers, scholars, and industry experts from around the globe have gathered here to delve into the cutting-edge advancements in robotics and mechatronics. This conference serves as a platform for the exchange of innovative ideas, fostering collaboration, and exploring the latest trends and challenges in the field.

Talks will delve into foundational aspects, algorithms, approaches, or experiences, focusing on novel or interesting insights, relating to any aspect of software engineering such as requirements, design, development, analysis, testing, verification, or deployment.

With a focus on interdisciplinary collaboration and technological innovation, this conference promises to be an enriching experience for all attendees. From keynote speeches by renowned experts to interactive sessions and paper presentations, the program is designed to inspire, educate, and spark fruitful discussions that will shape the future of robotics and mechatronics.

Venue: Amrita Viswa Vidyapeetham, Amritapuri Campus,  Clappana P.O., Pin – 690525 Kollam, Kerala, India 

As you immerse yourself in the vibrant atmosphere of this conference, we encourage you to seize the opportunity to network with fellow researchers, forge new partnerships, and gain valuable insights that will propel your work forward. Additionally, take some time to explore the captivating beauty of Kerala, known for its lush greenery, tranquil backwaters, and rich cultural heritage.

On behalf of the organizing committee, we extend our heartfelt gratitude to all sponsors, supporters, and contributors who have made this event possible. Together, let us embark on a journey of discovery, innovation, and collaboration at the International Conference on Robotics and Mechatronics 2024..

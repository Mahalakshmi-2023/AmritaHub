Kochi: Amrita Viswa Vidhyapeetham

Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham has an extensive campus in Kochi, which includes several schools and institutes. Let’s delve into the details: Amrita Institute of Medical Sciences (AIMS): A premier multispecialty hospital. Known for its phenomenal growth in just 22 years. Committed to excellence in healthcare delivery, education, and research1. School of Medicine: Offers undergraduate and postgraduate medical programs. Provides comprehensive training to future doctors.

School of Dentistry: Focuses on dental education and research. Equips students with the skills needed for oral health care. School of Nursing: Offers B.Sc. in Nursing and Post Basic B.Sc. in Nursing. Prepares compassionate and skilled nurses.

In summary, Amrita’s Kochi campus is a vibrant mix of medicine, science, and technology, where students receive top-notch education and contribute to healthcare and research.

Counselors Conference 2024

Pathways to Student Empowerment: Cultivating Resilience, Creativity, and Innovation in a Fast-Changing World

Faridabad

Overview

As we navigate through unprecedented times, the role of school counselors has become increasingly crucial in providing essential support to students facing various challenges. The School Counselors Conference 2024 will feature engaging keynote presentations, theme-based talks, and networking opportunities, all designed to enhance your professional skills and empower you to make a positive impact within your school communities.

Participant Profile: Principal / Counselor

Program Venue: Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Amrita Hospitals, Delhi NCR Campus, Faridabad.

Registration Fee: Free of Cost

what you will learn

Here’s a brief overview of the sessions

  • Mindset Matters: The Transformative Power of a Growth Mindset Discover the profound impact of adopting a growth mindset on student achievement and well-being. Learn practical strategies to cultivate a growth mindset in both students and yourself, fostering a culture of resilience, perseverance, and continuous learning.
  • Future Ready: Fostering 21st Century Skills for a Flat World Unlock the key skills and competencies essential for success in the 21st century. Discover innovative approaches to teaching and learning that cultivate critical thinking, creativity, communication, collaboration, and adaptability, preparing students to thrive in an interconnected and rapidly changing global landscape.
  • The Evolving Social Landscape: Strategies for Counselling Teens in a Changing Dynamic Social Landscape Navigate the complexities of counselling teens in today’s evolving social landscape.

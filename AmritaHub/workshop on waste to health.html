About

The “Waste to Wealth” workshop will explore cutting-edge technologies and innovative applications across various fields, focusing on advanced techniques and sustainable solutions. Key topics include the use of radiofrequency heating for industrial applications and catalytic pyrolysis methods for producing upgraded bio-oil from biomass. Participants will also delve into biogas production from organic waste, highlighting its role in sustainable energy. The workshop will address predictive techniques for managing faults in arc plasma systems, enhancing smelting process efficiency. Additionally, sessions will cover AI and robotics in precision agriculture, emphasizing their impact on optimizing crop production and resource management. Finally, discussions on thermal energy storage systems will examine effective waste heat recovery methods, contributing to the overall energy efficiency and sustainability.
